## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- Floats a 4,000 t vessel out of a 200 m building dock: level-by-level draft, displacement, GM and aground/afloat state from the as-built weight model.
- Runnable via `cargo run -p drydock-flooding-sequence`.
